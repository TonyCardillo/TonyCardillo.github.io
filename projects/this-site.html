Overview

I like building my own tools, and entmoot.io is no exception. This site runs from a custom-built static site generator I made in Node.js.

Features

  • Markdown-based content with automatic HTML generation
  • Support for blog posts and standalone pages
  • Single command builds entire site

Design

The site aims for simplicity, performance, and maintainability.

  • Simplicity: clean monochrome theme with glassomorphic tiles
  • Performance: people and patients love efficiency, so I seek to make my projects fast
  • Maintainability: simple systems make maintenance easy on my two neurons
